### Twenty-five years of research on ceramides, lipotoxicity and insulin resistance: from lipid accumulation to a compartmentalized view of lipid signaling.
- American journal of physiology. Endocrinology and metabolism · 2026-Nov-09 · Eric Hajduch, Hervé Le Stunff
- PMID: [43004778](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43004778/) · DOI: 10.1152/ajpendo.90046.2026

This perspective suggests that ceramide-induced insulin resistance in skeletal muscle should no longer be considered merely a consequence of the total accumulation of intracellular ceramides. On the contrary, recent studies show that the role of ceramides in lipotoxicity depends not only on the molecular species involved, but also on their subcellular localization, their intracellular transport, and their ability to locally disrupt insulin signaling within specialized submembrane domains. This compartmentalized approach could help reconcile conflicting observations in this field and guide the development of more selective therapeutic strategies targeting harmful ceramide pools.
